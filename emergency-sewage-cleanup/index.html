Why Night and Weekend Response Matters

Every hour sewage sits, more of it soaks into drywall, baseboards and wood framing. Bacteria grow quickly, and in the warm, humid months common in North Texas, mold can start on wet materials within 24 to 48 hours.

Waiting until Monday can turn a job that needed a few feet of drywall removed into one that needs the whole room gutted. Fast response saves you money and keeps more of your home or business intact.
