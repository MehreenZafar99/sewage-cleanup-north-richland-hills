Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Sewage Backup?

Many standard homeowners policies do not automatically cover damage from a sewer or drain backup. Coverage is often added through a separate rider called a water backup endorsement (it may also be called sewer and drain backup coverage).

Every policy is different. Check your declarations page or call your agent.

When the City Main Is the Cause

If the backup came from a blockage in the public sewer main, not your private line, the city may handle the line. Whether the city pays for damage depends on its policies. Report it to the North Richland Hills Public Works Utility Division, keep records, and still file with your own insurance.
