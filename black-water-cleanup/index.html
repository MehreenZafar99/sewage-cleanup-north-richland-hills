Black Water Cleanup in North Richland Hills

Black water is the most dangerous type of water damage. It is any water carrying sewage, harmful bacteria, chemicals or outside runoff. In the industry it is called Category 3 water, and it needs to be handled with protective equipment, removal of affected materials and full disinfection.
