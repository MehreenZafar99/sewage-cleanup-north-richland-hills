Why Neighborhood Matters for Sewage Cleanup

Age of the sewer line. Clay and cast iron lines in older areas fail differently from PVC lines in newer areas. Roots and corrosion are common in older lines. Grease, wipes and sagging pipes are more common in newer ones.

Foundation type. Slab homes show a backup at the lowest drain. Pier and beam homes can hide sewage in the crawl space for days.

Trees. Neighborhoods with large, mature trees have more root intrusion into lateral lines.
