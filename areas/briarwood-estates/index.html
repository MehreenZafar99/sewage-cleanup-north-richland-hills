Open Floor Plans Spread Damage Further

Many remodels take out walls to create open kitchens and living areas. Without walls to slow it down, a backup can spread across a much larger area of flooring. Continuous flooring also makes matching harder, because a patch in the middle of an open room is easy to see.

When an exact match is not possible, we document it so you can discuss continuous flooring replacement with your insurance adjuster.
