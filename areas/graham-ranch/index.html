Your Line vs. the City Main

Your private line (lateral) runs from your house to the connection with the city sewer main. In most North Texas cities, the homeowner is responsible for this line, even the part that runs under the yard or sidewalk.

The city sewer main runs under the street and carries waste from many homes. The North Richland Hills Public Works Utility Division maintains the city's wastewater collection system.

Why Cleanup Should Not Wait

Even while the city or a plumber works on the line, sewage already in your home keeps soaking into materials. Start cleanup as soon as the flow stops. Keep a record of when you reported the problem, who you spoke with and what you were told.
