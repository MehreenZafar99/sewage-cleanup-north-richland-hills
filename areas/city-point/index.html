Why New Homes Still Get Sewage Backups

  • ✓ Construction debris. Drywall mud, grout, concrete and scraps of material can end up in drain lines during building and cause blockages months later.
  • ✓ Improper slope. If a line was not installed at the right grade, waste can settle instead of flowing out.
  • ✓ Settling. New homes settle as soil compacts, which can shift lines in the first few years.
  • ✓ Wipes and grease. New PVC still clogs when the wrong things go down the drain.
  • ✓ Shared lines. In attached townhomes, a blockage in a shared line can back up into more than one unit.

Builder Warranties

Many newer City Point homes are still under a builder warranty. If you have a sewage backup:

A plumber's report on the cause (debris, slope or a defect) can help with a warranty claim.

  • ✓ Stop using water and keep people out of the area.
  • ✓ Take photos before anything is moved.
  • ✓ Notify your builder under the warranty process, in writing.
  • ✓ Do not let the damage sit while waiting for a reply. Call us for cleanup so mold and further damage do not start. Keep our documentation for the builder.

Multi Story Townhomes

City Point townhomes often have two or three floors. An overflowing toilet on an upper floor can soak through the subfloor and come through the ceiling below, damaging drywall, insulation, light fixtures and flooring on the way down. We check every level below the source.
