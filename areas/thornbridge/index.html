Signs Water Reached the Floor Below

Do not poke a sagging ceiling. It can hold a lot of water and collapse. Keep people out from under it.

  • ✓ A brown or yellow stain on the ceiling under a bathroom
  • ✓ Sagging or bubbling ceiling drywall
  • ✓ Water dripping from a light fixture or vent (turn off power to that circuit)
  • ✓ A sewage smell in a downstairs room
  • ✓ Peeling paint on the downstairs ceiling

What Cleanup Looks Like on Two Floors

  • ✓ Upstairs: extraction, toilet removal if water got under it, removal of affected flooring and drywall, disinfection of the subfloor
  • ✓ Between floors: removal of wet ceiling drywall and insulation, cleaning and disinfecting the joists
  • ✓ Downstairs: extraction and cleanup of anything that dripped down, plus removal of affected flooring and furniture if soaked
  • ✓ Drying: both levels at the same time, with moisture readings in the joist space
  • ✓ Restoration: new ceiling drywall, texture, paint and light fixtures as needed
