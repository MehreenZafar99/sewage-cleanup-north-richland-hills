Renters: What To Do First

If you rent in Iron Horse and sewage comes into your home:

The building itself is usually the landlord's responsibility. Your personal belongings may be covered by renters insurance if your policy includes backup coverage.

  • ✓ Stop using water in the unit.
  • ✓ Tell your landlord or property manager right away, in writing if you can (a text or email works).
  • ✓ Take photos of the damage and your belongings.
  • ✓ Keep out of the affected area.
  • ✓ Call us if your landlord asks you to arrange cleanup, or give them our number: (817) 204-3891.

Landlords and Property Managers

A sewage backup in a rental needs fast action. Texas landlords are expected to make repairs that affect a tenant's health and safety, and sewage is a clear health hazard. We help landlords by:

  • ✓ Responding quickly so tenants can stay or return sooner
  • ✓ Documenting the cause and damage for insurance and tenant records
  • ✓ Giving a clear scope and price before major work
  • ✓ Handling the rebuild so the unit is ready to rent again

Apartments and Multi Unit Buildings

In apartments and duplexes, one blocked line can affect several units. A backup in an upper unit can leak through the floor into the unit below. We inspect neighboring units and ceilings so contamination is not missed, and we can phase cleanup to keep unaffected units occupied.
