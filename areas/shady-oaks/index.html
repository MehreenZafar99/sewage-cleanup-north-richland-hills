Why Older Adults Should Not Clean Up Sewage

Sewage carries bacteria, viruses and parasites. Older adults are more likely to get seriously ill from exposure, especially those with diabetes, heart or lung conditions, or a weakened immune system.

Cleanup is also physically risky:

If you are an older homeowner, stay out of the affected area and call us.

  • ✓ Wet, contaminated floors are slippery
  • ✓ Lifting soaked carpet, furniture and boxes is heavy work
  • ✓ Bending and kneeling for long periods can cause falls
  • ✓ Strong cleaning chemicals and sewer gas can irritate the lungs

Can They Stay in the Home?
