Hidden Leaks in Older Homes

In older homes, sewage problems are not always dramatic backups. Many start as small, slow leaks you cannot see:

Over weeks or months, these leaks soak subfloors, rot framing and fill a room with odor. By the time anyone notices, the damage is often bigger than a sudden backup would have caused.

  • ✓ A cracked drain pipe inside a wall that drips with every shower
  • ✓ A worn toilet wax ring that lets a little water out with every flush
  • ✓ A corroded pipe under the slab that seeps into the soil
  • ✓ A broken drain under a pier and beam home that drains into the crawl space

Signs of a Slow Sewage Leak

  • ✓ A sewage smell that comes and goes
  • ✓ Soft spots in the floor near a toilet or tub
  • ✓ Stains on walls near bathrooms
  • ✓ Peeling paint or swollen baseboards
  • ✓ More gnats or drain flies than usual
