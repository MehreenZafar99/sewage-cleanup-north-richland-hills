Can Roots Get Into Newer Sewer Lines?

PVC sewer pipe is much more resistant to roots than old clay pipe, because its joints are glued or sealed. But roots can still find a way in through:

As trees in a neighborhood like Forest Glenn grow bigger, their root systems spread further and dig deeper in search of water, especially during the dry Texas summers.

  • ✓ Cleanout fittings with loose or cracked caps
  • ✓ The connection to the city main, where your line meets older or different pipe
  • ✓ Joints that shifted as clay soil moved over the years
  • ✓ Cracks from settling, construction damage or heavy equipment

Signs Roots May Be Involved

If roots are involved, a plumber's camera inspection can show where they are getting in, so the line can be repaired instead of just cleared.

  • ✓ A backup that happens again a year or so after the line was cleared
  • ✓ Slow drains that get worse in late summer and fall
