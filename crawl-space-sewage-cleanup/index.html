Signs of Sewage in Your Crawl Space

Smell sewage but cannot see it? The problem may be under your floor. Call (817) 204-3891.

Most homes in North Richland Hills are built on concrete slabs. But some of the oldest houses in the city, especially in the southern neighborhoods near Loop 820 and Boulevard 26, sit on pier and beam foundations with a crawl space underneath.

In these homes, a cracked drain pipe or a separated joint can leak sewage directly into the crawl space. Because nobody looks under the house very often, the leak can go on for weeks before anyone notices.

  • ✓ A sewage or rotten egg smell inside the house, often stronger near floor vents or in humid weather
  • ✓ Smell that gets worse when the HVAC runs, if ducts run through the crawl space
  • ✓ Soft, sagging or stained floors
  • ✓ More flies, gnats or pests than usual

Why Crawl Space Sewage Is a Bigger Problem Than It Looks

It spreads into the soil. Sewage soaks into the dirt floor and contaminates it.

It ruins insulation. Fiberglass insulation between floor joists acts like a sponge.

It damages wood. Joists, beams and subfloor absorb moisture and bacteria. Over time this can lead to rot and mold.

Working in Tight Spaces

Crawl spaces are dark, low and often have only a small access door. Our crews use compact equipment, portable extractors and long hoses so the work can be done from inside the crawl space without tearing up your floors.
