Is Sewer Gas Dangerous?

Sewer gas contains hydrogen sulfide, which causes the rotten egg smell, along with methane and ammonia. In normal household amounts it is unpleasant but usually not dangerous. In higher concentrations or enclosed spaces like crawl spaces, it can cause headaches, nausea, dizziness and breathing problems. Methane can also be a fire risk in large amounts.

If the smell is very strong or people in the home feel sick, open windows, get everyone out and call us.
