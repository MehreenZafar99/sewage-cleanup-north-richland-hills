Cleaning vs. Sanitizing vs. Disinfecting

These words get used as if they mean the same thing. They do not:

After sewage, you need all three, in that order. Disinfectants work poorly on dirty surfaces, so cleaning has to come first.

  • ✓ Cleaning removes visible dirt and organic matter. It does not kill germs on its own.
  • ✓ Sanitizing lowers the number of germs to a level considered safe.
  • ✓ Disinfecting kills nearly all of the bacteria and viruses on a surface.
