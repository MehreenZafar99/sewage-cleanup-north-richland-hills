Step 2: Safety Check and Inspection

On arrival, the crew checks for electrical hazards and puts on protective gear: gloves, boots, suits and respirators.

Then we map the damage. Sewage travels further than it looks. Using moisture meters and thermal cameras, we check under flooring, inside wall cavities, beneath cabinets and in nearby rooms. On pier and beam homes we inspect the crawl space as well.

We show you what we found and explain what needs to be removed and what can be cleaned.

Step 3: Containment

We seal off the affected area with plastic sheeting and, when needed, set up negative air pressure with HEPA air scrubbers. This keeps contaminated air and particles from spreading into clean parts of the property. We also lay down floor protection along the path in and out.

If your HVAC system pulled in contaminated air, we shut it down and seal the vents in the work area.

Step 4: Extraction

Standing sewage is pumped out first, using truck mounted or portable extraction units depending on the volume. Solids are removed and bagged. Waste is disposed of properly, never washed into a yard or storm drain.
