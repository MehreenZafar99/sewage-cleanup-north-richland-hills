Why We Only Work in NRH

We do one thing and we do it in one city. Our crews clean up sewage in North Richland Hills, and nowhere else. That focus is the reason we can get to you fast and the reason we know the homes, streets and sewer problems of this city better than a company covering all of the Metroplex.

Big restoration franchises cover dozens of cities from one office. When you call, your job goes into a queue with jobs from Fort Worth, Arlington and Denton. A crew might be an hour or more away.

Keeping our service area to North Richland Hills means our trucks are never far from Davis Boulevard, Rufe Snow Drive or Precinct Line Road. When sewage is spreading across a floor, that difference in drive time is the difference between replacing a little drywall and replacing a whole room.

  • ✓ Many homes south of Loop 820 and along Boulevard 26 still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines that are prone to root intrusion and buildup.
  • ✓ A number of the oldest homes in the city sit on pier and beam foundations, so a broken drain can send sewage into the crawl space without anyone noticing for days.
  • ✓ Newer homes in the northern half of the city, near North Tarrant Parkway, are almost all slab built, so a backup tends to show up first in a shower or tub.
  • ✓ Expansive clay soil across the city moves with wet and dry seasons, which shifts and cracks sewer lines over time.
