Toilet Overflow Cleanup in North Richland Hills

Most people think a toilet overflow is a quick mop job. Sometimes it is. But overflow water does not stay on top of the tile. It runs under the vanity, soaks the base of the walls, slips under the baseboards and can travel into the hallway or bedroom next door. In a two story home it can drip through the ceiling below.

About this service

Clean Water or Sewage?

Not every overflow is the same:

If you are not sure, treat it as sewage.

  • ✓ Clean bowl, clear water: If the tank overflowed from a stuck fill valve and the bowl was clean, the water is lower risk. It still needs drying.
  • ✓ Bowl with urine or waste: This is Category 3 water, the same as a sewage backup. It needs removal and disinfection.
  • ✓ Overflow that sat for more than a day: Even clean water becomes contaminated as it sits and picks up bacteria from floors and walls.
