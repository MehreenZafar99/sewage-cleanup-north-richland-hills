Why You Should Not Use a Shop Vac

Many people reach for a wet dry vac. For sewage, that is a bad idea:

  • ✓ Most household vacs blow fine contaminated droplets out the exhaust and into the air you breathe
  • ✓ They have small tanks, so you end up carrying and dumping sewage repeatedly
  • ✓ Contaminated waste ends up in a yard, toilet or sink, which can spread the problem
  • ✓ They cannot pull sewage out of carpet pad or from under flooring

Details

Extraction on Slab vs. Pier and Beam Homes

Slab foundations, common in most of North Richland Hills, hold sewage on the surface. It spreads outward under flooring, behind baseboards and into neighboring rooms. Extraction focuses on the floor surface and under any flooring that is removed.

Pier and beam homes, found in some of the oldest parts of NRH, let sewage drain through gaps and down into the crawl space. Extraction may need to happen both inside the house and underneath it. See our crawl space sewage cleanup page.
