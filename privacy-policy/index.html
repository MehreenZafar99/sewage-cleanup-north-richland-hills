This privacy policy explains how this website handles information when you visit it or call the phone number listed on it.

Information We Collect

This website has no forms. We do not collect names, emails or addresses through the site.

Phone calls. When you call (817) 204-3891, your call may be routed through a call tracking service. That service may record your phone number, the time and length of the call, and in some cases the call audio for quality and training. Information you share on the call, such as your address and the details of your problem, is used to provide the service you asked for.

Website analytics. We may use tools such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console to understand how visitors use the site. These tools may collect data such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed and time on site. This data is used in summary form to improve the website.

Cookies. Analytics tools may set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How We Use Information
